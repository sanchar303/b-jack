Rules & Strategy

✕

Rules

Get closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. Face cards count as 10, Aces as 1 or 11. Natural Blackjack pays 3:2. Split Aces receive one card each and stand automatically.

Basic Strategy

Hard Totals

• 8 or less: Always Hit

• 9–11: Double if dealer 2–6

• 12–16: Stand if dealer 2–6, Hit if 7+

• 17+: Always Stand

Soft Totals (Ace)

• Soft 13–17: Hit / Double

• Soft 18: Stand vs 2-8, Hit vs 9-A

• Soft 19+: Always Stand

Pair Splits

• Always Split: Aces & 8s

• Never Split: 10s & 5s

• Split 2s, 3s, 7s vs dealer 2-7
